# CS330

1. How do I approach designing software?

When I begin developing software, I typically start with an in-depth analysis of the project requirements to gain a clear, high-level understanding of what I’m designing. Once that’s complete, I break the project down into individual components and organize them into categories for eventual task distribution. After that, I move on to the actual development of the necessary objects. I believe this approach will serve me well in future projects.  
   
2. How do I approach developing programs?

When I move on to the actual software development phase, I begin by creating a functional minimum viable product (MVP) to present to the client. This allows me to validate the concept and gather feedback to ensure the solution is tailored to the user’s needs. The rest of the development process is then spent iterating through additional development and feedback cycles.
